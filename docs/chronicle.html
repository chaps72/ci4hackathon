On July 24, 2026, HHS rescinded portions of its Title VI civil rights regulations to align with statutory text and Executive Order 14281, followed on August 4 by a related NSF nondiscrimination rule in federally assisted programs. These changes signal an evolving federal civil rights compliance landscape for grant-receiving institutions that the office should review against current policies.

AHRQ grant terminations
Beginning July 20, 2026, reports emerged that AHRQ had terminated dozens of patient care research grants, with cancellations initially reported at $109 million and subsequently escalating to over $131 million across 28 states. The freeze has reportedly halted patient safety research nationwide and raised broader fears about the future of U.S. health services research. For the research office, this represents a significant and growing threat to funding for healthcare quality and patient safety work spanning multiple states.

Keyword-based research grant terminations
The White House has admitted to using keyword-based lists to terminate billions of dollars' worth of California research grants, with subsequent reporting exposing the contents of these federal keyword lists. The revelations show that grant cancellations were driven by automated or list-based screening rather than case-by-case review, raising significant concerns for institutions whose funding may have been flagged by targeted terms.
