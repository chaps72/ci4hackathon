Notice of Public Hearing: NIAID Proposed Reorganization
🆕 New
Health and Human Services Department · 2026-08-31 · Federal Register
Pursuant to the NIH Reform Act of 2006 (42 U.S.C. 281(d)(4)), notice is hereby given that the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID) of the National Institutes of Health (NIH) will be accepting comments on a proposed reorganization involving the Division of Clinical Research (DCR).
Emory impact · HIGH severity
NIAID is a core funder of Emory's infectious disease and vaccine portfolio (Emory Vaccine Center, Hope Clinic, and infectious disease programs in the School of Medicine), and its Division of Clinical Research supports clinical trial networks Emory participates in. A reorganization of DCR could alter program contacts, award management, and the structure of clinical research funding mechanisms Emory relies on. The concrete effect is administrative and strategic uncertainty for NIAID-funded clinical trials rather than immediate funding loss.
SVPR office: The SVPR office should coordinate an institutional comment to the public hearing reflecting Emory's substantial NIAID clinical-research stake and monitor for disruptions to DCR-managed awards.
