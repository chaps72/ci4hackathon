NIH Grant Terminations Disrupt 1 in 30 Clinical Trials, Impacting Over 74,000 Participants
American Journal of Managed Care (AJMC) · 2026-08-17 · press coverage
A study found NIH grant terminations earlier in 2025 disrupted about 3.5% of active clinical trials—affecting more than 74,000 participants and a $1.81 billion funding loss. The National Institute of Mental Health and the National Institute on Minority Health and Health Disparities had the most terminated grants.
