🔁 Ongoing: Analyses of Trump administration censorship and conditioning of science funding
Grant Witness · 2026-11-16 · press coverage
An analysis conducted with The Guardian found that the rate of revisions to NIH grant award text spiked in 2025 and 2026, with language related to health-equity research, diversity and inclusion increasingly removed. The authors report outright grant terminations have slowed since 2025 even as text edits rose.
Emory impact · MEDIUM severity

Investigators may find award text altered, affecting scope, aims framing, and reporting. The slowdown in outright terminations offers modest reassurance, but silent edits create compliance ambiguity.
SVPR office: The SVPR office should own tracking of any unilateral edits to Emory award text and establish a process for PIs to flag and document changes.
